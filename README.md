# Ali Zaidi E-Commerce Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze end-to-end e-commerce sales performance — from category-level breakdowns to regional distribution and year-over-year trends.

## 📊 Overview

This dashboard consolidates raw e-commerce transaction data into a single, decision-ready view for stakeholders to monitor sales, profit, and operational performance at a glance. It was designed to answer core business questions: *Which categories drive the most revenue? Where are our customers located? How does this year compare to last?*

## 🎯 Key Metrics (KPIs)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | $2M |
| Profit on Sale | $175K |
| Quantity Sold | 22K units |
| Avg. Delivery Days | 4 days |

## 🔍 Features

- **Regional Filtering** — Slice data instantly by Central, East, South, or West region
- **Sales by Category & Sub-Category** — Horizontal bar breakdowns (Office Supplies, Technology, Furniture / Phones, Chairs, Binders)
- **Monthly Sales by YoY** — Area chart comparing 2019 vs 2020 performance month-by-month
- **Monthly Sales by Profit** — Tracks profit trends alongside sales across the year
- **Geo-Mapping** — Bubble map visualizing sales concentration across US states/cities
- **Sales by Segment** — Donut chart split across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office
- **Sales by Payment Mode** — Donut chart comparing Cards, COD, and Online payments
- **Sales by Ship Mode** — Standard, Second, First, and Same Day class breakdown

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Power BI Desktop** — dashboard design & visualization
- **DAX** — calculated measures (Sales, Profit, YoY comparisons)
- **Power Query** — data cleaning & transformation
- **Data Modeling** — relational schema across category, region, and time dimensions

## 💡 Insights Delivered

- Identified Office Supplies as the top revenue-generating category
- Highlighted seasonal sales spikes toward Q4 (Oct–Dec)
- Revealed Consumer segment as the largest contributor (48% of sales)
- Showed Cash-on-Delivery (COD) as the leading payment method (43%)

2. Open in Power BI Desktop
3. Refresh data connection to your source (or use the included sample dataset)
4. Interact with filters/slicers to explore by region, segment, or category
